Sing&Scroll Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026 · Android app “Sing&Scroll”

In short

  • Your voice is processed on your phone and is never recorded, stored, or sent anywhere.
  • Song texts, your library, and settings stay on your phone.
  • If the app crashes, a technical crash report is sent automatically. It contains no song texts, logs, or audio.
  • A diagnostic log leaves your phone only when you tap a send button in the ⋯ menu.
  • No ads, no accounts, no tracking, no selling of data.

What Sing&Scroll does

Sing&Scroll shows the lyrics and chords of a song and scrolls them automatically while you sing. To know where you are in the song, it listens to your voice through the microphone and recognizes the words with a speech-recognition model that runs entirely on your phone.

Microphone

The app asks for microphone access the first time you tap Start. The sound is used only while you are singing, and only to recognize words and to tell singing from silence. Audio is processed in memory on your phone. It is not saved to a file, not uploaded, and not shared with anyone. When you tap Stop, listening ends.

Data stored on your phone

DataWhy
The song you pastedSo it is still there when you reopen the app.
Your library: saved songs, titles, artist foldersSo you can find and reopen songs.
Settings, such as text sizeSo you don’t have to set them again.
Diagnostic logs of the last 10 app runsTo understand problems if you report one. They include the song text and the words the app recognized, but no audio.
An error journalA short list of errors the app noticed.

All of this stays in the app’s own storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Data that leaves your phone

Crash reports. If the app crashes, a crash report is sent to Firebase Crashlytics, a service by Google, the next time you open the app. It contains the technical details of the error (where in the code it happened), your phone model, Android version, app version, and a random installation identifier that is not linked to your name or account. It does not contain song texts, logs, your library, or audio. Google processes this data under the Firebase privacy terms.

Logs you choose to send. The ⋯ menu has ways to send a diagnostic log, and each works only when you tap it:

  • Email log to the developer opens your email app with the developer’s address and the log attached. Nothing is sent until you press Send in your email app.
  • Share log… opens Android’s share window, and the log goes only where you choose.
  • Test versions of the app may also offer Send log to the developer, which uploads the log file to the developer through a Telegram bot. The file passes through Telegram’s servers under the Telegram privacy policy.

Nothing else is sent. The app has no ads, no analytics, no user accounts, and does not sell or share personal data.

Children

Sing&Scroll is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

  • You can deny or revoke microphone access in Android settings. The app will show the lyrics but won’t scroll them automatically.
  • You can delete songs from the library in the app, or clear all app data in Android settings.
  • To ask for crash reports linked to your installation to be deleted, email the developer at the address below.
